Subject: Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) Requirement Deadline - SR-DTC-2025-009 
Following the Important Notice 21985-25, informing of the amendments to The Depository Trust 
Company (“DTC”) Rules & Procedures (“DTC Rules”) relating to Legal Entity Identifier (“LEI”) 
requirements, this notice is to notify DTC Participants that the deadline to provide a valid LEI is 
December 12th, 2026. This notice applies to DTC Participants and does not apply to DTC Limited 
Participants or CDS Participants.     
The Rules provide that Participants that do not have a LEI on file with DTC shall have 60 calendar 
days from the date that Participants are notified by Important Notice to submit their LEIs. Failure 
to provide a valid LEI within 60 calendar days from the date that Participants are notified by 
Important Notice could subject the Participant to disciplinary sanctions, including a fine. 
DTC uses the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation ("GLEIF") as a reference source for LEI 
information and encourages Participants to ensure their LEI records in GLEIF are accurate and 
up to date.  
Information about obtaining a LEI can be found in the LEI-FAQ 
Action Required:  
Provide the following information to dtccmembershipdocs@dtcc.com   
• Your DTC account number  
• A valid LEI  
• Legal Entity Name 
• Country and State of Incorporation 
• Date of Formation (month and year) 
• Principal Place of Business Address (city and country)
