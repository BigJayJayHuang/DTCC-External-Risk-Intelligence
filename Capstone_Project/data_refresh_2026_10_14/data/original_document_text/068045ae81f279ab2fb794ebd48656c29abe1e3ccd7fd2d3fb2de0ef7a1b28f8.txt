IMPORTANT NOTICE 
Fixed Income Clearing Corporation - GOV 
DTCC Public (White) 
 
GOV #: GOV2248-26 
Date: October 12, 2026 
To: All Government Securities Division Members 
Category: Service Update 
Subject: FICC Veterans Day Holiday Schedule 
 
The Government Securities Division (“GSD”) of the Fixed Income Clearing Corporation 
(“FICC”) will be closed Wednesday, November 11, 2026, in observance of Veterans Day. The 
following changes in FICC's operations and schedules will be in effect: 
 
Government Securities Division RTTM, Comparison, and Netting Systems: 
 
Comparison System 
All Regular Way, When-Issued, and Forward Settling Trades executed on Tuesday, 
November 10, 2026, should be submitted on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, by the normal 
cut-off time of 8:00 PM EST. 
 
No trade input will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Trades executed on 
Wednesday, November 11, 2026, should be submitted on Thursday, November 12, 2026, 
along with trades of that day. 
 
Netting System 
All netted obligations resulting from compared Regular Way Trades among Netting Members 
submitted on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, will settle on Thursday, November 12, 2026. 
 
All output will be available by 2:00 AM EST, Wednesday, November 11, 2026.  
 
Sponsored GC Service 
No trade input will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. 
 
 
GCF Repo and CCIT Services 
No trade input will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.
