Subject: Required Settlement System Updates and Key Milestones Towards November 2027 
On September 10, 2026, DTC issued Important Notice B24776-26, which advised all DTC 
Participants and any third-party service providers, service bureaus or other entities that 
connect to DTC on behalf of, or for the benefit of, one or more Participants (collectively, 
“Covered Entities”) that they must complete the required updates to their systems before the 
effective date of DTC’s settlement modernization. That notice further advised that the effective 
date would be in November 2027, with the specific date communicated through a subsequent 
Important Notice. 
DTC hereby announces that the effective date of DTC’s settlement modernization will be 
Monday, November 8, 2027 (the “Effective Date”). Implementation and cutover activities 
are expected to occur over the weekend of November 6 and November 7, 2027, in 
preparation for the production launch on the Effective Date. 
Beginning on the Effective Date, all Covered Entities must be operationally connected to and 
use DTC’s modernized settlement services. The modernized services will include changes to 
connectivity, data delivery, messaging, reporting, settlement workflows, and settlement 
profiles. 
As stated in Important Notice B24776-26, any Covered Entity that has not completed the 
Required Settlement System Updates by the Effective Date will not be able to access DTC’s 
settlement services. Failure to complete the required Covered Entity testing activities in 
accordance with the required timeline in advance of the Effective Date may also result in 
adverse action by DTC, as applicable, including sanctions, and the reporting of non-
compliance to relevant U.S. securities and banking regulators. 
Required Covered Entity Testing Activities 
All Covered Entities must complete all testing activities relevant to their organization, operating 
model, and use of DTC settlement services, including the following activities:

• Complete all required onboarding to DTC’s testing and production environments to 
begin testing and prepare for production launch. 
• Update messaging interfaces and processing to support the applicable future-state

• Update systems and procedures to consume modernized reports and data outputs. 
• Validate the readiness of service bureaus, vendors and other third parties relied upon

• Update operational procedures, controls, exception-management processes, business-

• Complete a network circuit capacity assessment and make any required bandwidth 
upgrades before the modernization go-live, so connectivity can support expected 
production volumes, including ISO 20022 messaging. 
• Complete production-readiness certification and attestation requirements communicated 
by DTC (more information on this requirement will be provided in upcoming Important 
Notices).

Optional Migration Period 
On November 13, 2026, Cover Entities may begin migrating to the new ISO 20022 transaction 
message format supporting current settlement functionality, in production, prior to the Effective 
Date. Covered Entities planning to migrate to production prior to the Effective Date must test 
ISO 20022 message formats and applicable scenarios, including providing DTC with an 
attestation of the requisite testing, prior to activation. DTC will close the migration window 
sometime in Q3 2027, to finalize preparations for the full transition and launch of DTC 
settlement modernization. 
Additional Information 
Additional information on DTC settlement modernization is available on the DTCC Learning 
Center, under the Settlement Release page, including testing documents, message 
specifications, reporting specifications, and the Transformation Integration Series webinars. 
The Required Settlement System Updates are described in detail on the DTCC Transformation 
site, where Covered Entities can find the Functional Change Document, Settlement Milestone 
Client Checklist, Client Testing Framework and Scripts, Transformation Functionality Client 
Checklist, and Transformation Insight Series webinars. 
Covered Entities that have any questions about the Required Settlement System Updates, 
activity milestones or readiness-monitoring process, or planning to leverage the ISO 20022 
migration period should reach out immediately to DTCCTransformation@dtcc.com or their 
DTCC Relationship Manager. 
 
Please note that the launch of auto-partial settlement functionality, including optional partial 
settlement and related partial delivery authorization, will not be on the Effective Date but, 
instead, will be at a later date. More information on auto-partial settlement will be provided in 
an upcoming Important Notice.
