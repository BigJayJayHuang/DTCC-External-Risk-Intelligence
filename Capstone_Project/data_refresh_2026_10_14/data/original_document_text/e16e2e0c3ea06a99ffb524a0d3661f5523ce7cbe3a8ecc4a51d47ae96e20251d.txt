ESMA appoints Carlos San Basilio as the new member of its Management Board
About ESMA
Management Board
09/10/2026
The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), the European Union’s financial markets regulator and supervisor, has appointed Carlos San Basilio of Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (CNMV), as the new member of its
Management Board
.
Mr San Basilio will be replacing Carlo Comporti of Commissione Nazionale per le Società e la Borsa (CONSOB),
who will be stepping down from his position as a member of the Board to
assume his new role as Chair of ESMA
, effective 1 November.
The election took place at the Board of Supervisors meeting in Paris on 6 October 2026.
From
1 November 2026
, the
Management Board will consist of:
Carlo Comporti, European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA);
Thorsten Pötzsch, Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin), Germany
Carlos San Basilio, Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (CNMV), Spain
Marie-Anne Barbat-Layani, Autorité Des Marchés Financiers (AMF), France
Christopher P. Buttigieg, the Malta Financial Services Authority (MFSA), Malta
Peter Tkáč, Národná Banka Slovenska (NBS), Slovakia
Armi Taipale, Finanssivalvonta (FIN-FSA), Finland
Natasha Cazenave, ESMA Executive Director (non-voting member)
Vojtěch Belling, ESMA Vice-Chair (observer)
European Commission representative (non-voting member).
The Management Board, chaired by the Chair of ESMA, is responsible for ensuring that the Authority carries out its mission and performs the tasks assigned to it under its founding Regulation.
