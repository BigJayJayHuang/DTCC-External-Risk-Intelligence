Direct Registration System Transformation (DRS)  
 
On November 21, 2024, through Important Notice B#21022-24, The Depository Trust Company (“DTC”) 
announced its next phase of DTC’s multi-year, multi-phase Securities Processing Transformation 
program, Direct Registration System (“DRS”) transformation.   
 
The transformation of the DRS system, pending regulatory approval, includes DRS Transaction 
Processing and DRS Centralized Billing. 
 
DRS Profile Deposits, DRS Withdrawal by Transfer and DRS Centralized Billing will transition to the 
SPA system on November 13, 2026.  
 
DRS Centralized Billing 
 
DRS Reject Centralized Billing is being expanded to include centralized billing for DRS Profile Deposits, 
DRS Deliver Orders (“DO’s”) and DRS Withdrawal by Transfer transactions and will become a 
mandatory use service, on the SPA system. 
 
These fees are currently invoiced by FAST transfer agents to Participants outside DTC. The expansion 
will digitize collection and remittance and reduce the operational burden of manual invoicing. 
 
FAST transfer agents will be required to establish their fees on SPA prior to DRS processing 
transitioning to SPA.  Please enter all DRS fees by November 5
th, 2026.  
 
How it works 
 
Entitlements 
 
Obtain the DRS Centralized Billing user entitlements.   
 
Access and entitlements to the SPA system must be provisioned by the user’s designated Super 
Access Coordinator (“SAC”). If SACs have any questions about how to provision access through the 
DTCC Portal in the Customer Registration System (CRS), they can email DTCIntegration@dtcc.com for 
assistance.
