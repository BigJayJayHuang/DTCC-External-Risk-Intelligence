ESMA analysis shows continued growth in cross-border investment services
Investor protection
14/10/2026
The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), the EU’s financial markets regulator and supervisor, has completed its
annual analysis of the cross-border provision of investment services
, together with national competent authorities (NCAs).
The exercise collected data from investment firms across 30 jurisdictions in the EU/EEA and found that cross-border investment activity continued to grow significantly in 2025.
Key findings:
A total of
379 financial firms provided cross-border services to retail clients.
Approximately 15.5 million clients in the EU/EEA received investment services from firms located in other Member States, almost twice as many as in
2023
.
Compared to 2024
:
The number of firms increased by 2%.
The number of retail clients rose by 47%.
Complaints remained stable (decreased by -0.2%).
Cyprus remained the primary location for firms providing cross-border investment services in the EU/EEA, accounting for 20% of passporting firms, followed by Luxembourg (14%) and Germany (12%).
France, Spain, Germany and Italy are the main destinations for retail clients receiving cross-border services in other Member States.
These findings will support ESMA and the NCAs in monitoring cross-border investment services and understanding developments affecting retail investors across the EU/EEA.
Next steps
ESMA will carry out the next data collection in 2027.
