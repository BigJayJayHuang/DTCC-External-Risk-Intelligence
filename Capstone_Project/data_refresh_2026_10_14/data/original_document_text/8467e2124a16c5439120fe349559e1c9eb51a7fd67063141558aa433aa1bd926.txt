Subject: INSURANCE & RETIREMENT SOLUTIONS - DECOMMISSIONING SOAP-BASED 
XML SERVICES 
 
To provide clients with a more secure, resilient, and scalable integration model while simplifying 
ongoing development and maintenance DTCC’s Insurance & Retirement Solutions (I&RS) plans to 
decommission SOAP-Based XML services in 2027.  This is sooner than originally anticipated to 
strengthen the security offered for these message types, reduce reliance on end-of-life technology and 
advance our modernization efforts. 
 
Clients using these SOAP-based XML services below should begin migrating to the corresponding 
REST/JSON services and complete testing and implementation before Thursday, November 18, 
2027.  
 
• Attachments 
• IFT Web Services: Withdrawals, Arrangements, Fund Transfers, Values Inquiry, Policy 
Administration, Policy Inquiry, and Death Notification 
 
Client action and service availability 
REST/JSON alternatives are either available today or planned before decommissioning. Clients should 
identify affected integrations, confirm replacement services, assess connectivity, security, and testing 
impacts, assign accountable business and technology owners, and allow sufficient time for PSE testing 
and production deployment. DTCC will communicate service-specific readiness and implementation 
guidance as milestones are confirmed. 
 
Operational impact: After November 18, 2027, the affected SOAP-based XML services will no longer 
be available. Clients that have not completed migration may experience disruption to related business 
processes. 
 
Support: For implementation support, submit a case through MyDTCC or Contact Us and include the 
affected service, anticipated timeline, and business and technical contacts.
