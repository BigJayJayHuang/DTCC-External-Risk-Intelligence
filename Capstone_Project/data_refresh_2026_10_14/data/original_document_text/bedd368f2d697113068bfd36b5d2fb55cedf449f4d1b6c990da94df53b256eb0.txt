IMPORTANT NOTICE 
Fixed Income Clearing Corporation - MBSD 
DTCC Public (White) 
 
MBS #: MBS1606-26 
Date: October 12, 2026 
To: All Mortgage-Backed Securities Division Members 
Category: Service Update 
Subject: FICC Veterans Day Holiday Schedule 
 
The Mortgage-Backed Securities Division (“MBSD”) of the Fixed Income Clearing Corporation 
(“FICC”) will be closed Wednesday, November 11, 2026, in observance of Veterans Day.  
The following changes in FICC's operations and schedules will be in effect: 
 
MBS Division Clearing, Pool Netting, and EPN Services: 
 
Users will receive output files from the Tuesday, November 10, 2026, evening pass by 2:00 
AM EST on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. 
 
Clearing System 
No trade input will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Trades executed on 
Wednesday, November 11, 2026, should be submitted on Thursday, November 12, 2026, 
along with trades for that day. The Trade Date field should show the actual trade date that the 
trade was executed. 
 
Pool Netting System 
No pool instructs will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.  
 
EPN System 
No EPN messages will be accepted on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.
