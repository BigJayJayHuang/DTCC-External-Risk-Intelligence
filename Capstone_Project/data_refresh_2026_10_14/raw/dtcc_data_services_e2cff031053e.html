Data Services

Unlock Your Post-Trade Intelligence

As the industry's post-trade market infrastructure, DTCC sits at the center of global financial markets, supporting the processing, reporting and servicing of transactions across the marketplace. This unique position provides distinct visibility into market activity, enabling us to deliver data, insights and intelligence that help firms better understand markets, monitor trends and navigate change with confidence. 

DTCC Data Services combines trusted industry data with flexible delivery and standardized formats to help firms reduce market friction, improve operational efficiency and enhance market observability. From reference and corporate actions data to market intelligence and analytics, our solutions help organizations turn post-trade data into actionable insight. Let our solutions help you spend less time reconciling data and more time acting on it.
