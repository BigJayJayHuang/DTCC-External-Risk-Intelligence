Trade Processing Solutions

Optimize Your Post-Trade Operations

As markets around the world move to T+1 settlement, the stakes have never been higher. Speed, precision and resilience in post-trade are no longer optional – they are essential.

Our trade processing solutions set the standard for post-trade excellence, automating every step of the post-trade lifecycle. We connect buy-side, sell-side and custodian firms of all sizes to counterparties across 85 markets globally, optimizing post-trade operations by eliminating manual processes and inefficiencies, and enabling fast and efficient settlement.

Explore Our Services

Our market-leading trade processing solutions automate every step of the post-trade lifecycle. With CTM®, ALERT® and TradeSuite ID®, you can eliminate manual, error-prone and inefficient processes and achieve a fast, streamlined and optimized end-to-end post-trade workflow. 

  • Trade Allocation & Matching: CTM®

    Optimize your post-trade operations with automated trade allocation, confirmation, matching and settlement 

    • Streamlined allocation, confirmation and matching of global transactions
    • Real-time view of positions across Equities, Fixed Income, Equity Options and Money Markets
    • Unrivalled connectivity to a vast network of global depositories and exchanges 
    Learn More

  • SSI Management: ALERT®

    Manage and share accurate Standing Settlement Instructions (SSIs) globally, with the industry’s largest SSI database

    • Securely store, centrally manage and automatically share accurate SSIs 
    • Access to over 17 million global multi-asset SSIs, including Cash/FX 
    • Real-time data validation ensures high data quality and reduces settlement risk
    Learn More

  • Trade Affirmation: TradeSuite ID®

    Automate trade affirmation between counterparties for DTC-eligible securities

    • Automated distribution of trade details for DTC-eligible securities with TradeSuite ID
    • TradeSuite ID supports the efficient processing of U.S. domestic trades
    • Eligibility is determined based on participant status and security type 
    Learn More

The Future of Trade Settlement

From paper-based securities settlement that took seven days to process, to a fast, automated and resilient post-trade lifecycle, over the past five decades trade settlement has been completely transformed. 

As global capital markets move to T+1 settlement, operational efficiency in post-trade is essential.
