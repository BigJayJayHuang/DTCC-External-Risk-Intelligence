Digital Assets

Connecting Traditional and Digital Markets

As financial markets evolve, DTCC is connecting established market infrastructure with emerging digital asset ecosystems. Securities held at DTC can be represented as digital assets and used across approved networks while remaining anchored to the protections and resiliency market participants rely on today, supporting interoperability and more efficient movement of value across traditional and digital environments. 

The Connective Tissue of Digital Finance

Co-authored by DTCC, Citi, and Swift, this white paper examines how interoperability and programmability can help reduce fragmentation, improve connectivity across financial markets and lay the foundation for institutional-scale adoption of digital assets.

Operational Efficiency

Built to Integrate

Governance and Control
