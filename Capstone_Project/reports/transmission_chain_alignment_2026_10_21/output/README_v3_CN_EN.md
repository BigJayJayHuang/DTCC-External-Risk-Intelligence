# Complete Transmission Chain Catalog v3

October 21, 2026. This release covers all 45 reference chains in the supplied DTCC document and retains 11 team extensions, totaling 56 routes. All 25 v2 routes and all 22 legacy mappings are preserved; 31 reference chains are added.

Business totals are 23 FICC, 26 ECS, 4 Asset Services, and 3 WIS. Additions are respectively 13, 11, 4, and 3. The two Word files provide the full Chinese and English catalog. The JSON registry includes all definitions, reference coverage, addition status, legacy evidence, and optional indicators. The two additions guides isolate the newly added entries and monitoring suggestions.

Every new chain is untested and its data readiness has not been assessed for that specific mechanism. Monitoring suggestions do not imply successful data collection or validation. No data collection, training, testing, or scoring was performed. Historical evidence remains the October 16, 2026 snapshot; v2 files and historical results are unchanged.

Join results using both definition version and chain ID. Reused reference IDs can describe mechanisms different from legacy v1 IDs and must not inherit old results. Asset Services and WIS retain the business classifications in the supplied reference.
