# Revised transmission chains

Definitions were revised on October 21, 2026, using the supplied DTCC three-step format. Similar chains follow the reference, conflicts defer to it, and independent mechanisms without conflicts remain.

The two Word documents contain Chinese and English versions of the chain catalog, complete legacy mapping, validation-transfer notes, and data guidance. The JSON registry provides 25 routes, 22 mappings, optional indicators, original status snapshots, and the complete reference catalog.

Fourteen routes use reference IDs and eleven use TEAM extension IDs. Four broader hypotheses were split by stage or actor; two rule-change entries were merged while preserving separate NSCC and DTC outcomes. Original files and historical results are unchanged.

No data collection or model run was performed in this revision. Evidence is the October 16, 2026 snapshot. Changed targets or scopes do not inherit legacy pass labels. Revised specifications require a separate freeze and validation. The S times V framework remains without E, and missing V is not zero.
