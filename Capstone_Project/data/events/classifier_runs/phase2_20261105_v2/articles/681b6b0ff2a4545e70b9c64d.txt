ePayments Systems Limited enters special administration

On 15 October 2026, ePayments Systems Limited (EPS) entered special administration. Mark Boast and Allister Manson of Opus Restructuring LLP (Opus) have been appointed as joint special administrators.

EPS is authorised by the FCA to issue e-money under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 (EMRs).See details on the Financial Services Register.Who to contactThe special administrators are responsible for managing customer claims against the firm and returning funds to customers where possible.If you have any questions about the administration process, please contact the special administrators:Email: epayments@opusllp.comWebsite: opusllp.comAs the firm continues to be authorised by us, we will engage with the special administrators and seek the best outcome for customers.
