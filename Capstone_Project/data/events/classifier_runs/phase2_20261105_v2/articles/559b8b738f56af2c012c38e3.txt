Chandler King (City) Limited enters creditors’ voluntary liquidation

On 17 September 2026, Chandler King (City) Limited (Chandler King) went into creditors’ voluntary liquidation. Grace Jones and Karyn Jones of Parker Andrews were appointed as joint liquidators.

Chandler King, an FCA-authorised firm, was an independent financial adviser which advised on investments and pensions. On 28 April 2026, Chandler King agreed to a voluntary requirement with the FCA, which restricted its activities. Before entering liquidation, Chandler King applied to cancel its FCA permissions, which is subject to review by the FCA.
