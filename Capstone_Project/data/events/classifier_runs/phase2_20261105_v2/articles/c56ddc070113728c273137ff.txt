Leeds office move

We have signed an agreement for a new office at 2 Wellington Place, which will become our long-term home in Leeds from 2028.

The new office will provide 36,097 sq ft of space for our growing Leeds presence.Our Leeds office, currently at 6 Queen Street, has continued to grow since opening in 2022.Securing a new permanent base reflects our commitment to the city and supports our 5-year strategy ambition to have 1,000 roles based outside London by 2030.There is still some time before the move, and we'll share more about our new office and plans closer to 2028.
