Proposed Agency Information Collection Activities; Comment Request

In accordance with the requirements of the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (PRA), the OCC, the Board, and the FDIC (the agencies) may not conduct or sponsor, and the respondent is not required to respond to, an information collection unless it displays a currently valid Office of Management and Budget (OMB) control number. The Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council (FFIEC), of which the agencies are members, has approved the agencies' publication for public comment of a proposal to revise and extend for three years the Consolidated Reports of Condition and Income (Call Report) (FFIEC 031, FFIEC 041, and FFIEC 051) which is currently an approved collection of information. The agencies are requesting comment on revisions to these collections related to an interim final rule issued by the FDIC that implements section 902 of the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act. The agencies received emergency approval from OMB for these revisions.
