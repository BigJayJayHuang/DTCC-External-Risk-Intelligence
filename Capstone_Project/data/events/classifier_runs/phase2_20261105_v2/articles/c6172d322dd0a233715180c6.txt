Bullseye Financial Ltd enters administration

On 23 October 2026, Bullseye Financial Ltd (Bullseye Financial) entered administration. Robert Young of Azets Holdings Limited and Matthew Haw of RSM UK Restructuring Advisory LLP were appointed as joint administrators.

The joint administrators are responsible for managing the affairs of the firm during the administration process.Bullseye Financial is authorised by the FCA.
